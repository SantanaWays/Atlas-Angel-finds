Five Checks Before You Buy Online

Use this quick checklist to reduce surprises and compare options fairly.

1. Check the seller

Review recent feedback, how long the seller has been active, and whether complaints mention the same problem repeatedly. Be cautious if the listing or seller details are vague.

2. Read the exact item details

Confirm size, model, material, compatibility, condition, what’s included, and whether the photos show the actual item or an example image.

3. Compare total cost

Add shipping and taxes. Check delivery estimates and whether a return label or restocking fee could cost extra.

4. Understand returns and warranty

Read the return window, who pays return shipping, exclusions, and any warranty limits before you place an order.

5. Look for useful, recent reviews

Prioritize reviews that describe real usage and include relevant details. Don’t rely on star ratings alone; patterns in reviews are more informative than one very positive or very negative comment.

Watch for red flags

  • Prices that seem implausibly low compared with multiple reputable sellers.
  • Pressure to pay outside the platform’s normal checkout.
  • Requests for gift cards, cryptocurrency, or unusual payment methods.
  • Copied product photos with little specific information.
